About the Game

Bug Space Invasion is a 2D Sci-Fi Tower Defense game. Players must strategically manage their battery power, build defensive turrets, and utilize unique weapons and tools to survive endless waves of hostile alien bugs across different planets.

Why We Request User Data

Bug Space Invasion requests access to your Google Play Games Services and hidden Google Drive App Data for one specific feature: Cloud Saves.

Authenticating allows the game to securely backup your unlocked levels, weapons, and crystal currency to Google's servers. This ensures you never lose your progress and can synchronize your game across multiple Android devices. We do not have access to your personal Google Drive files, only the specific hidden save file created by this game.
